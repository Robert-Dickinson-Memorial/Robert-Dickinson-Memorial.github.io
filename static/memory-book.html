Robert E. Dickinson (1940–2026)

Earth-system scientist, mentor, and friend

Robert Earl Dickinson helped change how humanity understands the living Earth.

His pioneering work brought vegetation and land-surface processes into climate models and helped establish the foundations of modern Earth-system science.

His deepest legacy lives in both the models that now describe a more complete Earth and the people he trained to ask better questions of it.
