Minnesota connection

The Chippewa National Forest

Robert grew up in Minnesota. The Chippewa National Forest project restores jack pine, black spruce, red pine, eastern white pine, and northern white cedar while strengthening wildlife habitat and forest resilience.

Questions

Before you dedicate

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. One Tree Planted states that donors receive a personalized tree certificate with the honoree’s name.

Can I include a personal message?

Yes. The dedication flow includes a custom Tree-Card message, image choice, and delivery date.

Will I know the exact tree location?

The gift supports native reforestation projects where trees are most needed. It represents a living forest tribute rather than a individually marked tree.

Is the donation tax-deductible?

One Tree Planted is a U.S. 501(c)(3). Tax treatment depends on your circumstances; keep the provider’s receipt for your records.
